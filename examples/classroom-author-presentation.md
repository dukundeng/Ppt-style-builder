# Example: Classroom Author Presentation

This example structure works well for a 15-slide classroom presentation about an author.

## Input

```text
Topic: Amy Tan
Audience: high school or undergraduate literature class
Language: English
Target slide count: 15
Reference style: literary classroom deck with dark background, large serif titles, vintage image treatment, and chapter divider pages
```

## Slide Structure

1. Cover: author name, portrait, course or presentation subtitle
2. Contents: four major sections
3. Section divider: Life
4. Early life and family background
5. Education, career path, and turning points
6. Section divider: Major Works
7. Representative books and publication timeline
8. Focus work: themes, characters, and reception
9. Section divider: Writing Style
10. Language, voice, and narrative structure
11. Recurring themes and cultural context
12. Section divider: Influence
13. Literary influence and classroom relevance
14. Discussion questions or key takeaways
15. Closing: thanks, sources, or Q&A

## Design Notes

- Use divider slides to create rhythm.
- Keep biographical slides concise.
- Use verified portraits and book covers.
- Treat generated images only as atmospheric backgrounds, not factual evidence.
- Preserve editability with text boxes, shapes, and image layers.
